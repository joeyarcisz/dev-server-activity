# Code of conduct

Dev Server Activity welcomes constructive participation from people of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Discuss the work, evidence, and technical tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that increase process-safety risk or expand the project's scope.
- Protect other people's privacy. Remove personal paths, client names, credentials, and private logs from public reports.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `intake@gearedlikeamachine.com`. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
